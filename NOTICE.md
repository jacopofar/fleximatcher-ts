The optional Italian lexicon artifacts (`data/wiktionary-it.jsonl.gz`, the
browser demo lexicon, and anything generated by `fleximatcher-wiktionary`) are
derived from Wiktionary content extracted with
[wiktextract](https://github.com/tatuylonen/wiktextract).  Those data
artifacts are **not** covered by the MIT License:

- Wiktionary text is available under the
  [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
  and the GNU Free Documentation License.
- wiktextract is distributed under the MIT License; its extracted output
  inherits the licensing of the source Wiktionary dump.
- If you redistribute a generated lexicon, keep this attribution and the
  license notice that ships with the source dump.

Generated artifacts also embed metadata about the dump date and builder
version so that the data can be traced back to a source snapshot.
